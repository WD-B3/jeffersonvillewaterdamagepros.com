About Jeffersonville Water Damage Pros

A Jeffersonville-based water damage restoration team that explains the scope before it starts the work.

☎ Call (812) 642-7696

Who we are

Jeffersonville Water Damage Pros is a water damage restoration business based at 1600 Veterans Parkway in Jeffersonville, Indiana. We take emergency calls for Jeffersonville and nearby Clark County towns, including Clarksville, Sellersburg, Memphis and Henryville.

We are licensed and insured. We do not list awards, ratings or certifications on this site that we cannot document, and we would rather say less than say something we cannot back up.

How we work

Every job follows the same order. We answer the call, ask where the water came from, give you safety steps and plan the visit. On site we extract standing water, map moisture with meters and set drying equipment. We take readings until the structure is dry and share photos and a written scope.

We explain the scope and price before work starts. We do not guess a number without understanding the situation, and we do not promise how an insurance claim will turn out. Coverage decisions belong to your insurer.

What we handle

Our work covers the full range of water losses, from a burst pipe and a water heater leak to flood damage, sewage backup and commercial water damage. When moisture has led to growth, we also provide mold remediation. The services page lists every service by cause.

Why local matters

Jeffersonville has older neighborhoods, combined sewers in the downtown core and homes along the Ohio River. Knowing the area helps us ask better questions on the phone and plan equipment before we arrive. Our service area page lists the towns and ZIP codes we cover.

What happens when you call

A person answers the emergency line. We ask for your address and where the water came from, whether it is clean water from a supply line or dirty water from a drain, sewer or flood. We ask how many rooms are wet and how long the water has been there.

Then we tell you what to do while you wait, such as shutting off the water, staying away from wet outlets and moving valuables to a dry place.

We do not promise a specific arrival time, because roads, weather and the number of calls change from day to day. We do promise to be straight with you about what we know and do not know, and to explain the scope and price before work starts.

What you receive

You receive photos of the damage and the work, moisture readings from the start to the finish of drying, and a written scope. These records help you talk with your insurance company, and they show that the structure was dried to normal levels. If wet materials have to be removed, we explain why and photograph them first.

What we do not do

We do not decide whether your insurer will pay. We do not do roofing, plumbing repair or electrical work, and we say so when a job needs those trades. We do not use contact forms or sell your information. If a job is outside what we do well, we tell you and point you to the right kind of company.

Questions we hear most

People ask whether they can dry things themselves, whether mold will follow and whether they should call their insurer first. The short answers are simple. Small clean-water spills can often be handled at home. Mold becomes likely when materials stay wet for more than a day or two.

And you should call your insurer and a restoration company at the same time, since drying should not wait on paperwork. Our services page and our guides on specific problems go into more detail.

Equipment and methods

Water extraction uses pumps and extraction units sized to the job. Drying uses air movers to push air across wet surfaces and dehumidifiers to pull moisture out of the air. Moisture meters and thermal imaging help find wet areas that are not visible. The right mix depends on the materials, the amount of water and how long it has been there, so we do not apply one setup to every home.

Daily visits check the readings and move equipment. The job ends when the numbers match unaffected areas, not when a set number of days has passed.

Our commitments

We keep our claims small and true. We tell you what we see, what it means and what we recommend. If drying is enough, we say so, and if materials must come out, we show you why. You decide what happens in your home, and you get the paperwork to back it up.

Questions are welcome at any stage, including after the job is finished. Keep our number handy, and save it in your phone before you ever need it.
