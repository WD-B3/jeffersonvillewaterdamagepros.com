Questions to Ask a Water Damage Restoration Company in Jeffersonville, IN

By the Jeffersonville Water Damage Pros team

You may have only minutes to choose. These questions help you tell a careful crew from a sales pitch.

☎ Call (812) 642-7696

After a flood or a burst pipe, you are stressed, the house is wet and you need help now. That is the worst time to compare companies. A short list of questions, asked over the phone, can tell you a lot about how a company works.

No question is a promise, and a good answer on the phone does not replace a written scope. But a company that explains its process clearly, avoids pressure and puts things in writing is usually a better choice than one that does not.

Questions about the process

  1. What will you do first when you arrive?
  2. How will you find out what is wet, and will you take moisture readings?
  3. What equipment will you use and how long will it stay?
  4. How will you decide what to remove and what to save?
  5. How will you tell when the structure is dry?

A good answer includes extraction, moisture mapping, drying equipment and documented readings. Our structural drying page describes what that looks like.

Questions about paperwork and money

  1. Will you give me a written scope before work begins?
  2. What is included, and what is billed separately?
  3. How do you handle changes to the scope?
  4. Will you share photos and readings for my insurance claim?
  5. Do you work directly with insurers, and who is responsible for payment?

Be cautious about any company that insists on a signature before explaining what it will do, or that promises your claim will be approved. Coverage decisions belong to your insurer.

Questions about qualifications

Ask whether the crew is licensed and insured as required for work in your area, and ask who will actually be in your home. Do not rely on a logo or a badge, and do not accept a vague answer. If a company claims a certification or a license number, you can ask for the details and check them with the issuing body.

We state only what we can document, and we do not make claims we cannot support. That is the standard we suggest you apply to any company, including us.

Red flags

  • Pressure to sign immediately.
  • A price quoted without anyone seeing the damage.
  • A promise that insurance will pay.
  • No written scope or no clear explanation of what will be done.
  • Refusal to share photos, readings or records.
  • Door-to-door offers after a storm from crews you cannot identify.

Storms bring out out-of-town crews. Some are fine, and some are not. Local references, a stable phone number and clear paperwork help you decide.

After you choose

Confirm the plan in writing, ask for the name of the lead technician and keep your notes from the first call. If something changes during the job, ask for the change to be written down before more work happens. Clear communication on both sides is the best protection you have against surprises.

What to have ready when you call

The more you can tell a company, the better its answer. Before you dial, gather a few facts so that the call goes quickly.

  • The address and a good phone number.
  • Where the water came from and whether it is stopped.
  • How much of the home is wet and which rooms.
  • How long the water has been there.
  • Whether the electricity is on in wet areas.
  • Your insurance company name and claim number, if you have one.

If the water is dirty, say so. If you are not sure, describe the smell and color. That helps the crew bring the right equipment and protection.

It also helps to keep a pen and paper by the phone. Write down the name of the person you speak to, what they say about the process and any timing they mention. Notes from the first call are useful if something changes later, and they make it easier to compare companies if you call more than one.

Local checks worth making in Jeffersonville

If your repair is large, the city of Jeffersonville says that repairs costing more than 50 percent of a building's market value, excluding land, mean the whole building must meet current floodplain standards. Ask your contractor whether permits are needed, and confirm with the Building Commissioner at (812) 285-6412.

You can also ask neighbors in your part of town, from Port Fulton to downtown, whom they have used, and check how long a company has served the area.

Frequently asked questions

How fast should a company respond?

Ask what they can do for your situation. We do not promise a specific arrival time, because it depends on conditions, and you should be wary of any company that does.

Should I get more than one quote?

If time allows, yes. In an emergency, ask your questions quickly and start extraction while you compare.

Do I need to be home?

Someone should be available for the first visit and to approve the scope.

Can I choose my own contractor?

Usually yes. Confirm with your insurer.

What if I am not satisfied?

Raise it with the company in writing first. Your insurer and state consumer offices are next steps.
