Emergency Commercial Water Damage Restoration in Jeffersonville, IN

When water hits a business, every day of downtime costs money. Learn how commercial water loss is handled in Jeffersonville.

☎ Call (812) 642-7696

Water damage in a business is more than wet floors. It can mean stopped sales, spoiled inventory, damaged records and tenants who cannot work. The goal of commercial restoration is to protect the building, limit downtime and keep documentation in order for the owner, the tenant and the insurer.

This page covers offices, retail, restaurants, multi-unit buildings and light industrial space. For a single-family home, start with water extraction or burst pipe cleanup.

Commercial water emergency: first steps

The first calls are often to the property manager and the plumber. Shut off water at the main or the zone valve, secure the area and protect electronics, inventory and records.

  1. Close the main or zone shut-off valve.
  2. Cut power to wet areas only if the panel is dry and safe to reach.
  3. Move inventory, files and equipment off the floor.
  4. Notify the property owner or manager and your insurance contact.
  5. Photograph everything, then call our line for extraction and drying.

What makes commercial work different

Large floor plates, suspended ceilings, carpet tile, shared walls and tenant spaces change how drying is planned. Water that starts in one suite can run into neighbors. Cleaning up in sections lets part of a building reopen while the rest dries.

Commercial spaces often have more than one stakeholder. A written scope and photo log help the owner, the tenant and the insurer agree on what was damaged and what was done.

Common commercial causes

  • A burst supply line or sprinkler head.
  • Roof leaks above suspended ceilings.
  • A restroom backup, which brings sewage cleanup into play.
  • Stormwater entering at doors and loading docks.
  • HVAC condensate overflow.

Keeping a business open while it dries

Drying equipment can run in unoccupied rooms while the rest of a space stays in use. Zoning, plastic containment and after-hours scheduling help keep revenue coming in.

Older downtown buildings can have mixed plumbing and several tenants, so tell the dispatcher about the building layout early in the call. The older combined sewer area, from Main Street west toward Clarksville, is where the city says heavy rain can overload the system, so floor drains there are worth watching.

Planning around your business hours

A shop, office or rental cannot always close for days. We ask what has to stay open, where customers walk, which equipment is critical and who authorizes work. The plan then divides the loss into zones so unaffected areas stay in use.

  • Photograph inventory, equipment and finishes before cleanup.
  • List the affected rooms and who occupies them.
  • Tell us about alarms, sprinklers and shutoffs.
  • Find out who holds the lease and the insurance policy.

For rentals, tell us whether the landlord, a tenant or both need records. We keep readings and photos that can go to either party.

Records for property managers and owners

Property managers need documentation they can forward. We provide dated photos, moisture readings by area and a written scope. That helps with insurer claims, tenant communication and repair scheduling.

Insurance and your policy

Commercial property policies often cover sudden water damage, while business interruption coverage is a separate section. Leases may also assign responsibility between landlord and tenant.

Ask your agent and review your lease. Keep invoices, photos and a log of lost business days.

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle commercial water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Jeffersonville?

Yes. Business water losses happen overnight and on weekends, and the dispatch line is answered 24/7. Call with the building address and the type of water, and a same-day response is arranged when a crew is available.

Can you work after hours?

Yes, scheduling around business hours is common. Tell the dispatcher your hours.

Do you handle multi-tenant buildings?

Yes. Work is documented by area so each tenant and the owner can see what was done.

Who pays, the landlord or tenant?

That depends on the lease and the cause. Your agent and the property owner can sort that out.

Will you help with documents for my insurer?

We provide photos, moisture readings and a written scope for your adjuster.
