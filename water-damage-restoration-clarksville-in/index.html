Emergency water damage help in Clarksville, 24/7

Water damage does not wait for business hours. Our emergency line takes calls at night, on weekends and on holidays. Emergency water removal starts with a call, and you can tell us what you see even if you are not sure what it means.

  • Call from the wet room if you can, so we can ask what you see.
  • Tell us the source: pipe, appliance, roof, storm or sewer.
  • Mention how long the water has been there.
  • Say whether anyone in the home is elderly, ill or has a baby, so we can plan around them.

Our water extraction service handles standing water first, and structural drying follows until the readings match dry areas.

What Clarksville homes see most often

Burst or leaking supply lines, water heater failures, washing machine hose bursts and storm water in basements are the usual causes. Each leaves water in different places, so the first step is working out where it traveled.

A local note on water service

Indiana American Water serves Clarksville, Jeffersonville and New Albany. On July 5, 2023, a main break in Clarksville led to a boil advisory for customers around West Washington Street, north McKinley Avenue and north Randolph Avenue. A water main break is a utility problem, but a line that fails inside your home is yours to deal with.

If your water was shut off or low pressure returns suddenly, check faucets and appliances for leaks once service is back. Air and pressure surges can loosen weak fittings.

Before We Arrive

Stop the source

Close the main valve or the valve at the fixture. Not sure where it is? Say so on the call and we will talk you through finding it.

Power and wet outlets

Leave the electrical panel alone if you would have to stand in water to reach it. Keep clear of wet outlets and power strips.

Lift what you can

Get rugs, small furniture, electronics and papers up off the wet floor in your Clarksville home.

Keep people out of dirty water

If it came from a drain or a flood, keep children and pets away from it and wash up after any contact.

Photograph first

Quick photos and a short video of the water line help your adjuster later.

Call the line

We answer at any hour. Give your address, the source and which rooms are wet.

What to expect on the first visit

On the first visit, the crew confirms the water source is stopped, removes standing water and checks walls, floors and ceilings with a meter. We explain which areas will be dried in place and which materials may need to come out, and we show you the readings.

Drying equipment stays on site for days. You will hear it, and you should not turn it off. A technician comes back to check readings, and you receive photos and a written scope as the work moves along.

Insurance, mold and what comes next

We take moisture readings and photos so your insurer has records. We cannot say what a policy will cover, and we do not promise outcomes. If wet materials sat for a few days, ask us about mold remediation. Homes in Charlestown (ZIP 47111) and other towns farther north can call the same line.

For repairs beyond drying, we explain what is a restoration task and what is a rebuild task, so you know who does what.
