What water damage looks like in a rural home

Rural and semi-rural homes may have crawl spaces, sump pumps, well pumps and septic systems. A failed sump pump or a wet crawl space can go unnoticed for days. Equipment in outbuildings can leak without anyone seeing.

Before We Arrive

Shut off water at the main

Homes on a well may also have a pump switch. Turn that off too if the pump is feeding the leak.

Mind the electricity

Well pumps, sump pumps and freezers draw power in wet areas. Switch them off only if you can do it from a dry spot.

Raise belongings

Lift items off floors in basements, crawl space entries and utility rooms.

Keep clear of dirty water

Septic or drain overflow is not for bare hands. Keep family and animals out of it.

Take pictures

Photograph the damage and the source so the record exists before cleanup begins.

Call us any time

Give a clear address and a nearby landmark, since rural roads can be hard to find at night.

County resources and rules

Memphis is not an incorporated town, so building and zoning questions go to Clark County Planning and Zoning. Ask them if your repair needs a permit, especially if the home is in a floodplain.

On March 2, 2012, an EF4 tornado crossed Clark County along about 17 miles of path, according to the National Weather Service. It reminds us that storms can open roofs and bring water into homes across the county, and that a roof tarp and prompt drying matter.

What to expect on the first visit

The crew confirms the source is stopped, removes standing water and takes meter readings in walls, floors and ceilings. If your home has a crawl space or a basement, we check there as well, since water settles in low areas.

Drying equipment runs for days, and a technician returns to check readings. You receive photos and a written scope, and we explain the price before work starts. Ask about permits with Clark County if the repair is large.

After extraction

Once the standing water is gone, drying begins. We place air movers and dehumidifiers, check readings and adjust until the structure is dry. If wet materials sit too long, we also handle mold remediation.
