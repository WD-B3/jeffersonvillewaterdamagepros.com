Storm water damage in Henryville

After a storm, the roof, windows and siding may be damaged, and rain soaks insulation, ceilings and floors. A tarp on the roof stops more water from coming in, but the water already inside needs to be removed and dried.

  1. Make the home safe and stay clear of downed lines.
  2. Cover openings with tarps if it is safe to do so.
  3. Photograph the damage for your insurer.
  4. Call us to extract and dry wet areas.
  5. Discuss roof repairs with a roofer.

Before We Arrive

Make the home safe

After a storm, check for downed lines and damaged walls before you go near wet areas.

Cover openings

If you can do so safely, cover a broken window or a roof opening to slow the rain.

Turn off wet power

Leave the panel alone if the room is wet and you are unsure. Tell us what you see.

Save what you can

Move bedding, papers and electronics to a dry room away from the opening.

Document storm damage

Photograph the roof damage, the wet ceiling and the floor for your insurer.

Ring the emergency line

Call even if power is out and tell us where the water is coming in.

Permits and county rules

Henryville is not an incorporated town. For permits, zoning and rebuild questions, ask Clark County Planning and Zoning. If your property is in a floodplain, ask about additional requirements before rebuilding.

Keep records of repairs. If your home was damaged by a storm that qualifies for assistance, saved photos and receipts can matter.

What to expect on the first visit

The crew confirms the water is stopped, removes standing water and takes meter readings in ceilings, walls and floors, which is where storm water tends to travel. If the roof is open, we note it for your roofer and insurer.

Drying equipment runs for days, and a technician returns to check readings. You receive photos, readings and a written scope. We explain the price before work starts, and we do not guess without seeing the damage.

Keep receipts for tarps, temporary repairs and lodging if you have to leave. They matter for your claim, and the sooner you collect them in one place the easier the paperwork becomes later.

Drying and rebuilding

After extraction, we place air movers and dehumidifiers and check moisture readings. We explain which tasks are drying and which are rebuild tasks, and we point you to the roofer or contractor for structural repair.

If you are near Sellersburg or Memphis, the same crew can serve you. We also handle mold remediation when wet materials were left too long.
