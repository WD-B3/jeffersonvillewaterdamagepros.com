Emergency Water Heater Leak Cleanup in Jeffersonville, IN

A failed tank can empty 40 gallons or more across a floor. Learn how to shut it down and how cleanup and drying are handled in Jeffersonville.

☎ Call (812) 642-7696

A water heater leak is one of the most common ways a house floods, and it often happens while no one is watching. Tanks corrode from the inside, and when the lining finally fails the water empties onto the floor in a short time. Most residential tanks hold between 30 and 80 gallons, which is enough to soak a finished basement or a first-floor closet.

This page covers tank failures and leaking connections at the heater itself. A pipe that runs to the heater and bursts is a burst pipe problem, and a leaking washing machine or dishwasher belongs to appliance leak damage.

Water heater leak emergency: shut it down

The heater has two things to turn off: the water going in and the power or gas feeding the burner or elements. Do both before you start cleaning.

  1. Turn off power at the breaker for an electric heater, or set a gas heater to pilot or off.
  2. Close the cold water supply valve on the pipe above the tank.
  3. Open a hot faucet somewhere in the house so the tank does not pull a vacuum while draining.
  4. Do not stand in water near the heater if the breaker is in the same wet area. Call the electric utility instead.
  5. Photograph the tank, its label and the wet floor.

If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility and 911 from outside.

Why tanks fail

Corrosion at the bottom of the tank is the usual cause, helped along by sediment. Leaks at the relief valve, the drain valve or the supply fittings are smaller but still need attention.

Local water chemistry matters. Groundwater supplied by Indiana American Water in the Jeffersonville area measured 182 ppm hardness in the utility's 2024 report, and hard water leaves scale on heater elements and tank bottoms. Flushing the tank on a schedule from the maker's manual helps slow that buildup.

What cleanup looks like

The crew extracts the water, checks the wall base and floor with a moisture meter and removes anything that cannot be dried. Heaters in basements and utility rooms usually sit on concrete, which dries well, but finished walls and flooring nearby may not.

Drying equipment, including air movers and an LGR dehumidifier, runs until moisture readings return to normal. Basement installations are common, so the basement cleanup page explains how finished spaces are handled.

Replacement and repair

Replacing the heater is a plumber's job, and many homeowners ask the plumber to add a drain pan and a leak sensor at the same time. A pan with a drain line limits the damage when the next tank fails.

If the heater is in a closet on an upper floor, water can reach the ceiling below. In that case see ceiling water damage repair.

Insurance and your policy

Sudden tank failure is often covered by homeowners insurance, while long-term seepage and maintenance failures are often not. The water heater itself is usually treated as a maintenance item and may not be covered.

Keep the old tank until your adjuster says otherwise, and keep the label with its age and model.

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle water heater leak cleanup emergencies at night or on weekends in Jeffersonville?

Yes. Water heaters tend to fail at night when the house is quiet, and the dispatch line is answered 24/7. Shut off power and water, call, and a same-day response is arranged when a crew is available.

How much water does a water heater hold?

Most home tanks hold between 30 and 80 gallons. Check the label on the side of yours.

Is it safe to touch a leaking heater?

Turn off power and water first. If water is near the breaker panel, stay out and call the electric utility.

How long does drying take?

On concrete, often a few days. Walls and carpet nearby may need longer. Moisture readings decide when to stop.

Should I replace or repair the heater?

A plumber can tell you. A leaking tank is normally replaced, while a leaking valve may be repaired.
